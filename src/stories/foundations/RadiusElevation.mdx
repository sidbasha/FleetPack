# Radius & Elevation

Shape and depth both communicate structure, not decoration. Rounder shapes
signal "grab this"; higher elevation signals "this floats above what's
beneath it." Both scales below are additive `rounded-r-*` / `shadow-e*`
utilities in `styles.css` — they don't touch Tailwind's own `rounded-lg`
etc., which ~90 existing call sites already depend on at different values;
migrating a component to the new scale happens per-component, not here.
